I. Title & Description of the project 

Wellthy
Wellthy is a VR project providing undergraduate COOP students in technical fields with a simulation of behavioral interviews, immersing the user in the same high-stakes circumstances as a real life interview.

a. What type of XR Project is it? 

Virtual Reality (VR)

b. Project features – what does the user do, what makes it interesting, etc. 

The user will…

Configure their interview experience based on
Number of questions 
Question type (behavioural or technical)
Interview session duration (5 minutes OR 15 minutes OR 30 minutes)
Be shown an interview question 
Begin the interview question
Indicate they have completed the interview question; either to finish the session or move onto the next question 
Complete a self-evaluation to reflect upon their experience
Interpret a rubric to enhance self-evaluation

What makes it interesting? 

building muscle memory
Experience real conversation dynamics 
Experience human pressures
Practicing in a safe, yet organic environment
